Privacy

See what stays on your TV and when a feature connects to another service.

Settings and records stay on the TV

There’s no DodoMeow TV account to create. Display preferences, configured services and device addresses, favorites, and learning progress are stored on your TV.

Voice and AI can run locally

The Full package recognizes speech on the TV when the built-in recognizer is selected. You can also connect a model you run yourself. Cloud speech, desktop AI assistants, and cloud chat, including the app’s own chat service when available, handle their respective requests.

Home devices connect over your network

Phone setup connects directly to your TV. Your NAS or camera is reached at the address you provide. Online live TV and on-demand video connect to their content sources.

You decide whether to send error reports

You can review reports stored on the TV in phone setup and send them manually when the reporting service is available. Automatic reporting is off by default. Exported settings backups can include your service keys, so keep them safe.

During testing, our server does not accept reports from the app. If something goes wrong, take a photo of the screen and email it with a short description to newtv@mail.grokbot.com.
